Adaptive Dark

For sites that are left alone but still look light, or whose own dark design you don't like.
On: all sites are darkened unless you switch one off above. Off: only sites you switch on.
On: sites are darkened only while Windows or Firefox is set to dark, and go back to normal when you switch to light. Off: always darkened.

Sites that already have their own dark design are left alone. Alt+Shift+D switches the current site on or off.
